SSRN's official guidance was checked on 9 October 2026:

- [Submission guidelines](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started)
  call for an English full-text PDF displaying the title, authors and affiliations,
  plus a written date, abstract and valid author contact details for submission.
- [PDF guidance](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/pdf-issues) calls for
  embedded fonts, selectable text and unrestricted access/copying. The generated
  PDF is unencrypted and retains its text layer; all fonts are embedded.
- [AI policy](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/AI) requires disclosure of
  material assistance and author verification of generated content before
  submission. The title page discloses Codex's assistance with implementation,
  data checks, execution, literature verification and manuscript preparation.
  Include that disclosure alongside the abstract in the submission form too.
- [Current affiliation guidance](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/affiliation)
  asks for a verified institutional affiliation. The draft's **Independent
  researcher** descriptor does not establish account eligibility. Confirm the
  appropriate affiliation and contact information in the author profile before
  submitting; no employer or institution has been attributed to this research.

This revision prepares the document for review. It does not attest to completed
author verification, SSRN acceptance or an upload. The author must review the
