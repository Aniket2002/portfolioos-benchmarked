\section{Introduction and motivation}
The research question concerns how tracking-error and turnover constraints change
signal expression, and how transaction costs affect realized net performance.
Signal capture is not investment alpha.
\section{Related literature}
Ledoit and Wolf's covariance shrinkage work motivates the existing risk estimator
\cite{lw}. Equal-weight allocation supplies a simple competing rule; the literature
examines optimized versus naive diversification \cite{dgu}. These references do
not validate this extension's economic performance.

\section{Mathematical methodology}
Scores combine winsorized cross-sectional standardized momentum, negative trailing
volatility and reversal, using only prices through the information date.
For daily return at $t$, the empirical runner requires information through at most
$t-2$ and models closing weight targets at $t-1$. This does not establish executable
share-order fills. Volatility states use expanding historical quantiles excluding
the current rolling volatility estimate; state thresholds never use future data.
\section{Portfolio optimization}
The existing convex program maximizes
\[
 s^\top w-\lambda_r(w-b)^\top\Sigma(w-b)
 -\lambda_T\lVert w-w_{\rm pre}\rVert_1
\]
subject to full investment, long-only positions and explicit position, sector,
tracking-error and half-L1 turnover caps. Scores are preferences, not expected
returns. Ten assets cannot support an 8\% position cap. No constraints are relaxed.
\section{Experimental design}
\textbf{Missing: final declared periods and grids.} The runner freezes scenarios
before loading data and defaults to validation. TE, turnover, cost, regime,
ablation and robustness comparisons must be declared before holdout evaluation.
Development and validation inform protocol design; holdout access must be disclosed.
\section{Empirical results}
\textbf{Not performed.} No empirical performance, confidence interval,
significance, alpha or market validation is claimed. Existing synthetic findings

\section{Robustness and limitations}
Synthetic integration checks cover input integrity, timing, drift and accounting.
They do not test market validity. Missing exchange-session checks, point-in-time
adjustment vintages, cash/share execution and independent universe validation
remain material limitations. Correlated overlapping signals and multiple scenario
comparisons preclude naive significance claims.
\section{Economic interpretation}
No economic conclusion is supported without historical evaluation. High signal
alignment may coexist with negative active returns; net costs may outweigh gross
returns. Failure or underperformance must remain visible.
\section{Conclusion}
The extension prepares an auditable empirical workflow. The study remains
incomplete pending the proposed specification and suitable historical inputs.
